What's free

Being someone else's accountability buddy is free, permanently. Linking, approving or declining requests, holding PINs, and viewing what a buddy shares with you never require a subscription. Basic task features remain free as described in the app.

Blocking, and its limits

App blocking uses Apple's Screen Time system. That system is Apple's, not ours, and it is not perfect: iOS may occasionally deliver a scheduled block or unblock late or not at all, and usage measurements are approximate. CrossOff is a tool to support your intentions, not a guarantee that an app can never be opened.

Phone calls and Emergency SOS are never blocked — Apple enforces that at the system level.

Please don't rely on CrossOff for anything safety critical. If you need to be reachable, don't block the way people reach you, and keep another way to be contacted.

If your subscription lapses, blocking turns itself off rather than leaving your phone restricted. Your buddies are notified when that happens.
